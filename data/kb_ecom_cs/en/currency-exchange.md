# Currency & Exchange Rates

Prices are shown in your region's local currency for reference. Actual charges are settled in TWD for Taiwan orders and USD for other regions.
If your card is in another currency, the final billed amount is converted by your issuing bank at its own daily rate and may differ slightly from the site display; your bank may also charge a foreign-transaction fee, which is charged by the bank, not by us.
The reference foreign-currency prices use the live rate plus a 1.5% conversion and are indicative only. Refunds are always returned in the originally charged currency.
