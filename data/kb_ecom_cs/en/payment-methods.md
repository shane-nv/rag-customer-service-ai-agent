# Accepted Payment Methods

We accept the following payment methods:
- Credit cards: Visa, Mastercard, JCB, American Express.
- Mobile payments: Apple Pay, Google Pay, LINE Pay.
- PayPal (available for cross-border orders).
- ATM transfer and convenience-store code payment (Taiwan only; must be paid within 3 days or the order is auto-canceled).
All card payments are processed by our payment provider with 3-D Secure encryption; we never store your full card number. For cross-border orders, we recommend a credit card or PayPal for better exchange rates and protection.
