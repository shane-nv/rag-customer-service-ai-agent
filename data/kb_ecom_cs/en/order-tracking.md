# Order Tracking

After dispatch you will receive an email and SMS with the tracking number. Track in real time via the Member Center under "My Orders" → "Track Shipment," or on the carrier's website using the number.
- Taiwan: T-cat (Black Cat) and 7-11 store pickup.
- Cross-border: DHL Express or EMS.
A few hours' delay in status updates is normal. If there is no update after 3 business days (5 for cross-border), contact support to trace the package.
