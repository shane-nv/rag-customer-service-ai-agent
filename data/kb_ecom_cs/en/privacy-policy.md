# Privacy & Data Use

We collect and use your personal data only as needed to provide our services, including your name, contact details, shipping address, and order and payment records. Payment information is encrypted by our payment partners; we do not store your full card number.
Your data may be shared with logistics, payment, and necessary service partners to fulfill orders, and we require them to protect it equivalently. Except where required by law, we do not sell your data or use it for purposes you have not consented to.
You may access, correct, or request deletion of your data, and unsubscribe from newsletters at any time. To exercise these rights or for privacy questions, contact support@miraloom.example. See the website's "Privacy Policy" page for full terms.
