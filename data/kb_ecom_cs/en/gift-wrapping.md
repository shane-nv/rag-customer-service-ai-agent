# Gift Wrapping & Gifts

We offer paid gift wrapping — select it under "Gift Options" at checkout and add a message card. Orders with gift wrapping have prices hidden on the packing slip so you can gift directly.
To send to the recipient's address, enter their name and address in the shipping details; billing remains under your account.
Promotional gifts ship with the order while supplies last; if the main item is returned, the included gift must be returned too, or its value is deducted from the refund. Gift-wrapping fees are non-refundable on returns unless the fault was ours.
