# Sizing & Fit Guide

Every apparel and footwear product page includes a size chart based on flat-measurement data (e.g., chest, length, shoulder width), with international size conversions (TW/JP/US/EU).
We suggest measuring a well-fitting garment you own and comparing, rather than relying only on your usual size, since fits (slim, relaxed) vary widely. For shoes, go by foot length in centimeters.
If you are between sizes, the "fit note" on the page indicates whether an item runs large or small. Still unsure? Share your height, weight, and usual size and support can advise. Wrong sizes can be exchanged under the return policy.
