# Exchange Process & Fees

Exchanges apply to swapping the same product for a different size or color. To switch to a different product, please return the item and place a new order.
Non-defective exchange: for domestic orders the first reshipment is free while return shipping is paid by the buyer; for cross-border exchanges, both legs are paid by the buyer.
Defective or mis-shipped exchange: both legs are free.
Exchanges depend on stock; if your desired option is out of stock, we will offer a refund or a restock wait. Exchanged items must also be new and unused with tags and packaging intact.
