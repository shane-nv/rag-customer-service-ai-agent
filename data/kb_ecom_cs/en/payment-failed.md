# Payment Failed or Declined

If your payment fails, common causes include: insufficient limit, exceeding a per-transaction cap, online or overseas transactions not enabled, a failed 3-D Secure check, or incorrect details.
We suggest: verifying your card details and expiry, contacting your issuing bank about any fraud hold, or trying another payment method.
Orders with failed payment are not created and will not ship. If you see an "authorization" on your bank statement but no order was created, it is usually a pre-authorization that your bank releases automatically within a few business days; we did not actually capture the charge.
