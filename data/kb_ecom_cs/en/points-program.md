# MiraLoom Points Program

Earn 1 MiraLoom Point per NT$100 spent; 1 point offsets NT$1 (cross-border orders use the USD equivalent). Points post after the order completes (past the return window).
Silver earns 1.2× and Gold 1.5× points. Points are valid for 12 months from posting and expire if unused; the system reminds you one month before expiry.
At checkout you can redeem points from a minimum of 100, with a cap of 50% of the order value per order. On returns, redeemed points are refunded and earned points are deducted accordingly. Points are non-transferable and cannot be exchanged for cash.
