# Refund Timeline & Methods

We initiate refunds within 1–3 business days after receiving and inspecting the returned item. Refunds are issued to the original payment method:
- Credit card: appears on your statement within 7–14 business days, depending on your issuing bank.
- LINE Pay / PayPal / mobile wallets: 3–7 business days.
- ATM transfer / convenience-store code: provide a refund account; funds are sent within 5–7 business days.
If MiraLoom Points were applied to the original order, they are returned to your account. Whether shipping is refunded depends on the reason: defects or mis-shipments are fully refunded including shipping; non-defective returns exclude shipping.
