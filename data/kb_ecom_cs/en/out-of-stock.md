# Out of Stock & Restock Alerts

If an item goes out of stock after you order due to inventory sync lag, we will notify you as soon as possible with options: wait for restock, swap for another item, or cancel that item for a refund.
For multi-item orders, we may ship in-stock items first and send the out-of-stock item once restocked, or handle it per your choice, with no extra shipping charged.
On the product page you can tap "Notify me when back in stock" to get an email when it returns. Restock quantities for popular items are limited; a notification does not reserve stock — availability is confirmed only at successful checkout.
