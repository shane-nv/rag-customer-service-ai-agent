# Creating an Account

You can register a MiraLoom account with an email address or mobile number, or link a Google, Apple, or LINE account for quick sign-in.
Once registered you become a general member, able to accumulate spending toward higher tiers and earn MiraLoom Points. After verifying your email on first sign-up, you receive new-member store credit (see the promotions page).
Each email can register only one account. If you see "this email is already registered," you likely created an account before — please sign in or reset your password instead. Registering means you agree to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
