# Membership Tiers & Benefits

MiraLoom has three membership tiers, based on your spending over the trailing 12 months:
- General: from registration, with basic shopping and points rewards.
- Silver: NT$10,000 cumulative — birthday credit and priority pre-orders.
- Gold: NT$30,000 cumulative — free shipping storewide, dedicated support, and early access to seasonal sales.
Upgrades are evaluated in real time after each completed order and re-assessed yearly on a rolling 12-month basis. Upgraded benefits apply immediately; if the threshold is not met the following year, the tier is adjusted accordingly. Points multipliers rise with tier — see "MiraLoom Points."
