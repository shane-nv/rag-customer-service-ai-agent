# Account Security & Personal Data

We protect your account and transaction data with encryption and will never ask by phone or SMS for your password, full card number, or one-time passcode (OTP).
If you receive a message claiming to be from us and asking you to operate an ATM or share a verification code, it is almost certainly a scam — do not comply and verify with official support.
We recommend enabling two-factor authentication, updating your password regularly, and avoiding staying signed in on shared devices. In the Member Center you can manage your data, review signed-in devices, and sign out of all sessions. For how we handle and store your data, see "Privacy & Data Use."
