# Return & Exchange Eligibility

You may request a return or exchange within 14 days of the delivery date. Items must be new and unused, with tags intact and original packaging, accessories, and free gifts included.
The following are not eligible: customized or engraved items, intimate apparel and swimwear, opened cosmetics or skincare, food items, and clearance items marked "final sale."
Defective or mis-shipped items are exempt from these restrictions and can be exchanged or refunded within 30 days. For cross-border orders, return shipping for non-defective items is paid by the buyer.
