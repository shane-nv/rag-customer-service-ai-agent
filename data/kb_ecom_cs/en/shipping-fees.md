# Shipping Fees & Free-Shipping Thresholds

Shipping fees depend on the destination. Free-shipping thresholds (per order, after discounts) are:
- Taiwan main island: NT$80, free over NT$1,500; outlying islands: NT$150, free over NT$2,500.
- Hong Kong, Macau, Singapore, Malaysia: from US$12, free over US$40.
- Japan: from ¥900, free over ¥8,000.
- United States, Canada, Australia: from US$18, free over US$60.
Gold members enjoy free shipping storewide. Fees are calculated at checkout based on your address and weight. Cross-border fees exclude import duties and taxes — see "Customs, Duties & Taxes."
