# Modifying or Canceling an Order

Orders can be modified or canceled before dispatch. Sign in to the Member Center and act on the order page as soon as possible, or contact support. You can change the shipping address and contact phone; to change items or quantities, cancel and reorder.
Once an order ships, it cannot be canceled — please follow the "Return & Exchange Policy" instead. For unpaid orders using a store code or ATM, simply skip payment and the system auto-cancels after the deadline.
For pre-orders or customized items, cancellation terms may differ — see the product page or contact support. Refunds for canceled paid orders follow "Refund Timeline & Methods."
