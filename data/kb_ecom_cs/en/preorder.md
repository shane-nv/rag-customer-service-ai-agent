# Pre-order Items

Items marked "Pre-order" have not yet arrived; the product page states the estimated ship date. If pre-order and in-stock items are in the same order, we ship everything together once the pre-order arrives; to ship separately, place separate orders.
The estimated ship date may shift with supply; we will email you the latest status. If the delay exceeds the period stated on the product page, you may keep waiting or cancel the pre-order for a full refund.
Pre-orders are covered by the return policy, with the window starting from the actual delivery date. Payment methods are the same as regular orders; some pre-orders require full prepayment.
