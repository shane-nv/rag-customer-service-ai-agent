# Materials & Care

The "Materials & Care" section on each product page lists the main composition (e.g., cotton, linen, wool, stainless steel) and washing/care instructions; follow them to extend the item's life.
Natural materials may vary slightly in color by batch and dye lot, and handmade items may differ slightly — this is a normal characteristic, not a defect.
For fabrics, we recommend cold hand-washing or a laundry bag, and avoiding bleach and tumble drying; for leather, avoid prolonged sun and moisture. If you are allergic to a material, read the composition or check with support before ordering. Wear from improper care is not covered by warranty.
