# Restock Notifications

If an item is out of stock or a size/color is sold out, tap "Notify me when back in stock" on the product page and make sure you are signed in or leave an email. When that variant is restocked, the system emails you automatically.
A restock alert does not reserve stock for you; popular items may sell out again quickly after the notice, so check out promptly once notified.
Some seasonal or collaboration items are not restocked once sold out, and the page will note "no restock." To confirm whether a specific item will return, contact support — we will share the latest we know, though actual availability depends on supply.
