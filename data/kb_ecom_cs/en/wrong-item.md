# Wrong or Missing Items

If you receive an item that does not match your order, or there are missing pieces, contact support within 7 days of delivery with photos and your order number.
Once verified as our fulfillment error, we will reship the correct item and collect the wrong one free of charge, with all shipping covered by MiraLoom.
If a free gift or accessory is missing, we will send it separately — no need to return the original item. To speed things up, please include a photo of the outer box label so we can trace the picking and logistics steps.
