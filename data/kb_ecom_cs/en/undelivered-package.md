# Undelivered or Lost Packages

If tracking shows "delivered" but you have not received the package, first check with housemates or building reception, and inspect your mailbox and surroundings.
If it is confirmed lost, contact support within 7 days of the "delivered" status. We will open a carrier investigation (about 3–5 business days domestically, 7–14 for cross-border).
Once loss is confirmed, we reship free of charge or issue a full refund. If delivery failed due to an incorrect address you provided, reshipment shipping is paid by the buyer.
