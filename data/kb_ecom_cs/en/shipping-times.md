# Delivery Times by Region

After dispatch, delivery times are as follows (business days, excluding weekends and public holidays):
- Taiwan main island: 1–3 business days; outlying islands (Penghu, Kinmen, Matsu): 3–5 business days.
- Hong Kong, Macau: 3–6 business days.
- Singapore, Malaysia: 4–7 business days.
- Japan: 4–7 business days.
- United States, Canada, Australia: 5–10 business days.
Orders usually ship within 1–2 business days of successful payment. During major sales, this may extend by 1–3 business days; actual arrival is per the tracking information.
