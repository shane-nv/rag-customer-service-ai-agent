# Order Confirmation

After checkout and successful payment, you receive an order confirmation email and order number; if you use ATM or a store code, you first receive an awaiting-payment notice with payment details.
If the confirmation does not arrive, check your spam folder and verify in the Member Center whether the order was created. If you were charged but no order appears, contact support with the charge time and amount.
An order confirmation means we have received your order; it is finalized upon successful payment and stock confirmation. In cases such as an obvious pricing error or stock shortage, we reserve the right to cancel and fully refund, and will notify you in advance.
