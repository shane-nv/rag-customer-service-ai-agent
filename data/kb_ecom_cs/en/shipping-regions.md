# Countries & Regions We Ship To

MiraLoom currently ships from Taiwan to the following regions: Taiwan (including outlying islands), Hong Kong, Macau, Singapore, Malaysia, Japan, the United States, Canada, and Australia.
Some items cannot be shipped to certain regions due to battery, liquid, or material restrictions; the system will notify you at checkout.
We are continually expanding coverage. If your region is not yet available, subscribe to our newsletter for updates. We currently do not support freight forwarding or shipping to third-party parcel-forwarding agents.
