# Using Promo Codes

At checkout, enter your code in the "Promo Code" field and apply it; the discount is reflected immediately. Usually only one code per order is allowed, and most codes cannot be combined with other discounts or member pricing — the campaign terms govern.
Common restrictions include: a minimum spend, specific items or categories, an expiry date, and a per-person usage limit. If it shows "invalid," check whether it has expired, the minimum is unmet, or it does not apply to items in your cart.
The discounted order total may affect free-shipping thresholds and earned points, which are calculated on the amount actually paid after discount. Codes cannot be exchanged for cash, and single-use codes cannot be reinstated if the order is canceled.
