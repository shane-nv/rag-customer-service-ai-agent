# Received a Defective or Damaged Item

If your item arrives defective, broken, or malfunctioning, contact us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and photos of both the product and the outer box.
We recommend filming the unboxing; if the damage occurred in transit, footage helps with the carrier claim.
Once confirmed, we will exchange the item free of charge; if no replacement stock is available, we issue a full refund including shipping. Both return and reshipment shipping are covered by MiraLoom — you pay nothing.
