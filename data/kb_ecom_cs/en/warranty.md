# Warranty

All items include a 14-day inspection period from delivery (non-defective returns allowed if eligible) and a 30-day defect warranty, during which manufacturing defects are exchanged or refunded free of charge.
Electronic items additionally carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering non-human-caused malfunctions; it excludes accidental damage, water ingress, drops, normal wear, and consumables.
To claim, provide your order number and photos or a description of the fault. Once confirmed eligible, we arrange repair, exchange, or refund. Warranty repairs for cross-border orders may require shipping back to Taiwan, with timelines and fees explained case by case.
