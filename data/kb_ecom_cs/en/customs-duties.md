# Customs, Duties & Taxes

Cross-border orders may incur import duties, consumption tax, or handling fees at the destination. These are the recipient's responsibility under local law and are not part of our shipping fee.
Amounts are assessed by local customs based on product category and declared value; we cannot pre-calculate or waive them. Some regions exempt shipments below a de minimis threshold.
To comply with regulations, we declare the true value and cannot under-declare. If a package is returned because it was not collected, the resulting shipping and fees will be deducted from your refund.
