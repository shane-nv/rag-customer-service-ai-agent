# Invoices & Receipts

Taiwan orders are issued an e-invoice, handled per your checkout setting (personal mobile-barcode carrier, donation, or company tax ID). The invoice is issued after dispatch and emailed to you; you can also view and download it in the Member Center.
For a company tax-ID invoice, enter the title and tax ID at checkout; it cannot be changed after dispatch.
Cross-border orders receive a commercial invoice for customs and expense records. For prize-winning e-invoices, we assist per e-invoice regulations. For a reissue or correction, contact support within the same invoicing period.
