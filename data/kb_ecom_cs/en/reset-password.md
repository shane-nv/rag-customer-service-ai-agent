# Forgot or Reset Password

If you forget your password, click "Forgot Password" on the sign-in page and enter your registered email; we will send a reset link valid for 30 minutes.
If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder and confirm you used the email from registration. If you signed up via social login, you may not have a separate password — use that social login instead.
For security, the reset link works only once. After resetting, all devices are signed out and must sign in with the new password. Use a password of at least 8 characters with letters and numbers, and avoid reusing passwords from other sites.
