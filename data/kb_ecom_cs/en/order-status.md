# Order Status Explained

Order status reflects current progress:
- Awaiting Payment: order created, payment not yet completed.
- Paid / Processing: payment succeeded, warehouse is preparing your items.
- Shipped: the package has been handed to the carrier and is trackable.
- Completed: delivered and past the return window.
- Canceled / Refunded: the order was canceled or the refund is done.
Status updates may lag briefly. If you were charged but the status stays "Awaiting Payment" for over 1 hour, contact support with the transaction time and we will reconcile the payment.
