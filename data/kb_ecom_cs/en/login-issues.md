# Login Issues

If you cannot sign in, check that your account (email/phone) is entered correctly, the case is right, Caps Lock is off, and your connection is stable.
If social login (Google/Apple/LINE) fails, confirm that the third-party account itself works and that it allows MiraLoom to access basic profile data.
After several wrong password attempts, the account may be temporarily locked for 15 minutes for security — try again later or reset your password. If you still cannot sign in, contact support with your registered email.
