# How to Request a Return or Exchange

Follow these steps:
1. Sign in to the Member Center, go to "My Orders," select the item, and click "Request Return/Exchange."
2. Choose a reason (not suitable / wrong size / defective, etc.); for defects, upload photos.
3. After submitting, you will receive a confirmation email and a return number (RMA).
4. For domestic orders we arrange T-cat doorstep pickup; for cross-border orders, ship the item back to the designated address within 7 days and enter the tracking number in the system.
Please keep the packing slip and original packaging. Once we receive and inspect the item, we will process your refund or ship the exchange within the timelines below.
