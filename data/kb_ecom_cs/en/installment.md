# Installment Payments

Orders of NT$3,000 or more paid with an eligible bank credit card can choose 3, 6, or 12 installments at checkout, currently limited to Taiwan-issued cards.
Approval and whether interest applies depend on each issuing bank's program; the plans we offer are usually bank-run interest-free or low-interest promotions, with final terms set by the bank.
If an installment order is returned, the refund follows the original installment method, and the bank cancels or offsets the remaining installments; timing depends on the bank. Installments are not available for cross-border orders or PayPal.
