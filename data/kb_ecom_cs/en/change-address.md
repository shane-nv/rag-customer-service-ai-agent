# Changing the Shipping Address

You can change the shipping address before the order ships: sign in to the Member Center as soon as possible and click "Edit Address" on the order page, or contact support.
Once an order has shipped, the address cannot be changed in our system. Some carriers (e.g., DHL) may offer limited redirection; we will do our best to relay the request but cannot guarantee success, and extra fees may apply.
If a wrong address causes a return, it is handled under "Undelivered or Lost Packages," with reshipment shipping paid by the buyer. Please confirm your address and recipient phone number at checkout.
